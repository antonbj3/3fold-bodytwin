**Measurements that can resolve the package's physical decision binders**

All assays below are proposed; none has been performed for this package. The starting point is X26's decision table (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X26/DECIDABILITY_TABLE.csv`): eight physical decisions are CLASS 2 when only existing inputs are refined. The ninth overarching example is an analytical crack-tip problem, not another empirical dental endpoint. A measurement resolves a binder only for the matched specimen, operator and regime. New data constitute an **information_link**; claims of a newly answerable physical question are **capability** claims. An **algorithm** claim would require a separate equally informed method comparison.

The rank is an assessment of distinct decisions addressed versus specimen preparation, instrument access, destructive work and follow-up time. Lab hours and monetary costs are UNKNOWN, so no numerical benefit-per-hour ratio is asserted. “Addressed” does not mean every dependency is closed. Shared observations retain their original identities and are not counted as independent evidence twice.

**1. Measure signed geometry and the assembled gap field.** Use a preparation with stable external fiducials and independently calibrated dimensions. Record the intended and as-built intaglio, physical insertion axis and local signed normal deviations; do not independently best-fit each surface and erase the seating error. Observe dry seating height, vertical marginal gap, 3D distance to the crown solid and marginal/axial/occlusal film separately. Preserve PER_POINT positions and normal directions; regional summaries are PER_SURFACE_REGION, and inference across specimens is POPULATION. Scanner error and contemporaneous seated gap are SIMULTANEOUS; the manufactured or dry state passed into cementation is HANDOVER.

[X13's contract](../../implementations/X13/README.md) specifies two calibration settings and a held dose/batch, starting at n=10 per setting. Implement two settings in batch A (20 crowns), a held third setting in A (10), and the same held setting in independent batch B (10): **40 total**, an explicit pilot layout rather than a powered equivalence trial. Measure all regions on each crown; three regions do not triple n. Fit only the calibration groups. Freeze predictions before revealing either validation arm. Retain the 20 µm regional error gate and the original 90% coverage requirement; a small pilot does not establish population coverage. At least three independent within-system laboratories per region remain necessary for the original transferable-inversion claim.

Use a locked replica, micro-CT or validated triple-scan operator. [ISO 12836:2015](https://www.iso.org/standard/68414.html) offers accuracy tests for mounted CAD/CAM digitizers; its scope excludes handheld and radiographic devices. Micro-CT therefore needs its own calibrated length/registration reference. For X10, [the existing request](../../implementations/X10/README.md) asks for a 5 µm reference bound on the signed wall-clearance measurement, with independent remaining-contact bounds. Its −44.19 µm decision crossing belongs to its 3° taper/40 µm-spacer scenario; it is not transferable to every crown.

**Freeze and discrimination:** X13's [lab_selfcal.py](../../implementations/X13/README.md) creates a new `FROZEN_LAB_PREDICTIONS.json` from calibration CSV and an outcome-free validation design. X10's [R4 freeze](../../implementations/X10/README.md) supplies a conditional decision witness; a different die needs a new operator, prediction and registration freeze. Held error above 20 µm rejects the local gap mapping. Accurate scanner geometry accompanied by incorrectly predicted lift rejects the contact/seating law. For a preselected absolute-error bound, n=59 independent specimens with no exceedances supports a <5% exceedance probability at one-sided 95% confidence; it does not give a deterministic whole-surface bound. Use each specimen's maximum over the predefined surface as one observation, including independent metrology uncertainty.

For squeeze flow, additionally measure the spatial film, vent boundaries, cement rheogram at the actual temperature/shear/time after mixing, and force–height–time with filling/outflow. Regional means cannot test the simulated 4.1098× conductance witness. Freeze a same-film pressure/seating prediction before its held dynamic test; **there is no validated physical hydraulic prediction in the present package**. Rheology calibration n is UNKNOWN; repeated time samples are not independent batches. Do not add predicted seating to a replica gap that already contains seating.

An optional shared metrology extension addresses X14: [its specification](../../implementations/X14/README.md) starts with **three identical posterior four-unit FDPs**, one at each disc height. Record product, shade, lot, radial/height position, enlargement factor, furnace/support schedule, pre/post signed shape and independently seated gap. Four margin coordinates test the planar model; full 3D intaglio is needed to test its physical applicability. Three is a scout assay, not validated n. Source STL/dimensions are missing, so exact reproduction needs those dimensions. After calibration, freeze signed angles, both chord-relative slopes and seated-gap intervals for new held pieces; [existing R5 predictions](../../implementations/X14/README.md) are conditional mathematical checks. An interval wholly below/above the declared 120 µm engineering threshold decides that scenario; overlap or unmeasured contact remains UNKNOWN. This adds a process test, not another X26 decision.

**2. Test the thickness/angle interaction with its fracture mechanism.** Follow [X1b LAB_PROTOCOL](../../implementations/X1B/README.md): the same STS molar form and 3Y zirconia batch, nominal 0.8/1.0/1.5 mm thicknesses, 0°/30° loading, **12 independent crowns per cell, 72 total**. Keep separate crowns for the two angles. Carry rank-1 scans into each specimen record. Measure die modulus on independent coupons, stiffness and complete force–displacement traces; document cement/support and defects, then blind-classify failure mode and fracture origin with two readers. Perfect bonding, fixed Hertz patches and a common batch-strength scale are closures to be tested. Material thickness and origins are PER_POINT/PER_SURFACE_REGION; force endpoints are PER_TOOTH. Manufacture/cement state → destructive load is HANDOVER.

Use the protocol's 5 mm steel ball and 0.5 mm/min crosshead rate; 5 N/60 s dry seating and die E=18±1.8 GPa are frozen protocol choices, not a crown-testing standard. [ISO 7500-1:2018](https://www.iso.org/standard/72572.html) addresses static testing-machine force calibration; it does not validate the dental contact model. Published comparators are [Chen Table 2](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma17020365) and [Lawson Tables 2–4](https://doi.org/10.3390/ma17051096), with different fixtures/supports.

**Freeze and discrimination:** archive [X1b FROZEN_PREDICTIONS](../../implementations/X1B/README.md), exact exports, batches and protocol before manufacture. Primary Q=(mean F_M2,30/mean F_D1,30)/(mean F_M2,0/mean F_D1,0). The model predicts **0.4743756**, operational window **0.351426–0.640340**; the separable control predicts 1, window **0.740818–1.349859**. A measurement 95% CI wholly outside a window rejects that model, wholly inside supports that scoped contrast, otherwise UNKNOWN. At assumed CV=0.20, X1b's delta-method calculation needs 7/group for 80% power against the control tolerance; the existing 12/group plan is retained. The secondary M1 contrast needs 86/group for that same target and is underpowered at 12. These counts cannot be replaced by X38's ordinary two-group t-test. More than 50% incompatible mode/origin in any cell gives FAIL_MECHANISM; unknown origin stays UNKNOWN, and incompatible minorities prevent a full mechanism PASS. A correct force with a wrong origin does not validate the model. This trial does not establish absolute load, fatigue life or clinical durability. To close X26's absolute-load binder, a successor must calibrate the matched strength/defect population, cement/support and contact law, then freeze absolute forces for separate held specimens; its validation n remains UNKNOWN. The 72-crown contrast is the available mechanism falsifier, not that completed calibration.

**3. Measure patch forces on the geometry used by the force model.** Fabricate a matched X18 virtual roof, with its exact support, thickness/material and geometry hash. At the frozen 100 N total, obtain registered nonnegative patch forces with simultaneous uncertainty, conservation of total force and uncertainty from sensor calibration/contact registration. Obtain an independently measured elastic response, such as registered DIC strain and load–displacement. Freeze that observation operator before loading; a surface strain is not automatically the unobserved interior tensile maximum. The current roof has no full preparation/cement/die. X1b requires a separate contact identification on its own specimens; neither force fractions nor tensors may be moved between the two tooth geometries by label alone.

**Freeze and discrimination:** X26 R3 (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X26/FROZEN_PREDICTIONS_R3.json`), force budgets (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X26/FORCE_MEASUREMENT_BUDGET.csv`) and `query_force.py` define the current consumer. For FDI 35/37/44/47, simultaneous absolute fraction errors ±0.20/0.20/0.10/0.20 around synthetic centers lower the conditional PER_POINT peak bounds from **194.819/245.269/127.365/203.687 MPa** to **67.328/64.647/90.508/49.862 MPa**. That can resolve the declared 100 MPa engineering test; actual measured centers must be recomputed, and actual strength is unknown. Require mesh refinement and the held elastic-response check before any physical stress claim. n for response-validation precision is UNKNOWN; first specify tolerances and specimen variance. X38's **26/design** is available only for a separate two-group continuous comparison with prespecified effect Δ/common SD=0.8, equal SD, normality and independent groups; it is not a force-sensor calibration or stress-accuracy guarantee. Force maps are PER_SURFACE_REGION, same-time responses SIMULTANEOUS.

**4. Acquire anatomy and realized pose independently.** For X5, use mandibular specimens with known reference fiducials, CBCT and an independent nerve/canal boundary from dissection and/or validated high-resolution imaging. For X20, use posterior maxillary specimens with separate bony sinus floor, crest and lateral surfaces; follow [the three-specimen pilot port](../../implementations/X20/README.md). A printed ToothFairy2 label shape checks fabrication but cannot establish anatomical truth. Freeze segmentation, query pose, full-body and relevant drill geometry before exposing reference distances. Measure signed local boundary error and whole-surface minima; geometry begins PER_POINT, and the consumer aggregates to PER_SURFACE_REGION. Planned → realized insertion is HANDOVER; simultaneous surface comparison is SIMULTANEOUS.

**Freeze and discrimination:** new specimens require new freezes using X20 `predict_local_case.py`; [its existing lab freeze](../../implementations/X20/README.md) and X5's [minimum port](../../implementations/X5/README.md) are templates/contracts, not predictions for new anatomy. Error beyond X20's inherited ±0.60 mm box rejects that universal scenario. External method precedents are [Veyre-Goulet](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1708-8208.2008.00083.x) for matched sections/caliper and [Timock](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22051495/) for CBCT/dissection; neither supplies a bound for the present scanner or canal.

Guide placement requires signed **planned minimum minus realized minimum**, rather than apex-error magnitude. X31's port (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X31/MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md`) gives **59 independent blocks per fixed guide/region** for a maximum-based 95% population-content/95% confidence tolerance bound, **86 per guide** for four simultaneously reported guides, or **113 per cell** for sixteen guide/region cells. These integers also reproduce in X38's binomial calculator at target exceedance risk 0.05. Independence and anatomical transfer need evidence; repeated teeth/sites in one specimen do not automatically multiply n. This can measure a geometric tail, not nerve-injury risk. Bone-edge measurements also supply X15's baseline, but do not measure remodeling.

**5. Separate attenuation calibration from bone mechanics.** Follow X24's minimum contract (historical external artifact: `../LANE_X24_CBCT_HU_CALIBRATION/MINIMUM_MEASUREMENT_CONTRACT.json`): certified HA at 0/0.1/0.3 g/cm³ for fit, 0.2 held out, measured water/air, and three non-collinear positions under the proposed spatial calibration. These are calibration levels/positions, not independent specimen counts. Acquire matched directional mandibular coupons, measure modulus in a prespecified strain range plus density/fabric, and retain cortical/cancellous identity. Phantom accuracy is PER_SURFACE_REGION; local mechanical observations must stay at their measured support. Scanner/model/physical comparison is SIMULTANEOUS.

**Freeze and discrimination:** X24 R3 (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X24/FROZEN_PREDICTIONS_R3.json`) records the failed global calibration. Freeze a new phantom/bone-law fit before a held density, position/protocol and independent bone-specimen test. The old 40 HU_ref holdout gate failed at 146.51 HU_ref; under the inherited closure, 5% E accuracy would require about 2.19–7.83 HU_ref. That is a PHENOMENOLOGICAL debt, not a physical modulus bound. Passing phantom error alone cannot validate E. [Misch mandibular compression](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0278-2391(99)90437-8) is a population comparator, not paired local truth. Bone-law validation n is UNKNOWN until tolerance and variance are specified; X38's 26/group applies only to the separate assumed d=0.8 comparison above.

**6. Measure the response to an actual trajectory over time.** X15 needs same-site bone/root reference surfaces, independently measured tooth movement and later bone/dehiscence observations with their elapsed times. [Existing measurement targets](../../implementations/X15/README.md) predict annotation geometry only. A new trajectory-to-response law and its endpoint must be frozen before held follow-up; **no current biological prediction can be validated by measuring a static phantom**. This is PER_SURFACE_REGION HANDOVER, with longitudinal clusters retained. Validation n and expected biological effect are UNKNOWN. It is ranked last because geometry cannot close this missing response law and the present package offers no identified biological discriminator. No subject recruitment or intervention is part of this handoff.

A separate X31 tissue assay can use anonymous extracted posterior teeth, common-frame micro-CT/sections and blind horn/axis/DEJ marking before/after a defined reduction. Record T, E and r on the same ray; remaining dentin is max(0,T−max(E,r)). A verified dentin cut surface supplies the smaller local observation E≤r, reducing this to max(0,T−r). X31 R4 (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X31/FROZEN_R4_PREDICTIONS.json`) is conditional; new landmarks require a new prediction/operator freeze. Sampling and physical boundary error must distinguish the 0.5/1 mm engineering queries; no identified validation n or biological-risk curve exists. A PER_POINT material observation requires coverage of all relevant horns before a PER_TOOTH minimum.

**Use the specimen counter for the question it answers.** From the package root:

```bash
./batch7/demos/X38/labcount plan --risk .1 --confidence .95 --true-risk .1
./batch7/demos/X38/labcount plan --risk .1 --confidence .95 --family-tests 4
./batch7/demos/X38/labcount plan --risk .05 --confidence .95
./batch7/demos/X38/labcount plan --risk .05 --confidence .95 --family-tests 4
./batch7/demos/X38/labcount plan --risk .05 --confidence .95 --family-tests 16
./batch7/demos/X38/labcount compare --endpoint continuous --effect .8 --power .8
./batch7/demos/X38/labcount compare --endpoint binary --p-a .30 --p-b .10 --power .8
```

The first two commands return **29** complete zero-failure specimens at one fixed condition or **42/condition** for four simultaneous conditions, respectively. This tests <10% horizon failure risk at one-sided 95% confidence, not comparative power. At true risk 10%, probability of zero failures in 29 is only **4.71%**. For a two-design comparison at assumed failure probabilities 30% versus 10%, two-sided Fisher α=0.05/80% power needs **69/design, 138 total**. The normal equal-SD d=0.8 comparison needs **26/design, 52 total**. These are POPULATION planning assumptions, not measured gains. Risk counts require a fixed load, configuration, population and complete horizon endpoints; unknown early censoring remains unknown.

For implant–abutment fatigue, use X25's frozen lab predictions (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X25/FROZEN_LAB_PREDICTIONS.json`) only after matching configuration, material, preload, lever arm, medium, frequency and temperature. [ISO 14801:2016](https://www.iso.org/standard/61997.html) addresses dynamic tests of specified endosseous implant assemblies; it is not a crown static-fracture protocol or an in-vivo performance prediction. The 5×10⁶-cycle endpoint here belongs to the published dataset/declared model. For early stopping, X25 requires its separately frozen order and sequential boundary; ordinary repeated 95% checks are invalid. Full fatigue work follows geometry/contact identification, rather than substituting for it.

**Freeze, rejection and return to the model.** Keep an immutable manifest of specimen IDs, exact CAD/SDF/export hashes, units, material/process batches, predictions, code, instrument references, observation operators, uncertainty and UTC time before the validation outcome is seen. Calibration outcomes may identify a new closure; they must not also become its held validation. Existing literature/demo freezes are often retrospective or conditional. The 40-crown extension and qualitative rank here are proposed designs, not frozen physical predictions. Every numerical value remains at its measured resolution; every PHENOMENOLOGICAL closure records the missing replacement measurement.

Before measurement intake, require the relevant controls to reject a bad unit/hash/registration, a wrong force fraction, a censored endpoint treated as a survivor, or a correct force with an incompatible fracture origin. Existing fault evidence is in [X1b](../../implementations/X1B/README.md), [X13](../../implementations/X13/README.md), [X14](../../implementations/X14/README.md), X26 (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X26/VERIFICATION.json`) and X38 review (historical external artifact: `reviews/XREV_batch7_pkg/RESULTS.md`); a new observation port needs its own actual rejection probe. Report all failures, unknown endpoints and rejected specimens by cause and denominator, including pre-test breakage. Compare held observations with the unchanged prediction first, then create a new model version and a new freeze before the next validation batch. Controls on these information links compare current defaults without the new measurement; equivalent arithmetic controls verify numerical integrity separately.
