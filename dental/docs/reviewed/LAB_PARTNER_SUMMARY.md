**What the dental package lets a prosthetics laboratory test**

The package provides local mesh/SDF construction, virtual crown modification, elastic simulations, design comparisons and checked exports. It identifies measurements that could turn these computations into specimen-specific predictions. No manufactured crown has completed the prediction → manufacture → measurement → model-improvement loop in this package.

The evidence below combines published measurements, independent numerical recounts and declared simulations. “Runnable” means the documented local Linux environment with its pinned dependencies; a new laboratory installation remains unverified. From this directory, `./run_all.sh X1B X13 X14 X26 X31 X32 X38` runs the relevant demonstrations; each linked README identifies its inputs, figures and reference data.

New information enters at the interfaces. A fixed spacer does not supply a measured regional film. Equal regional means can hide a simulated 4.11× difference in hydraulic conductance when narrow film sections connect differently. A global sintering factor does not supply signed distortion or seated contact. A total force does not supply force fractions at contact patches. These observations can change the design question; replacing their absence with finer mesh sampling cannot determine them.

X26 (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X26/README_DEMO.md`) names eight physical decisions, all CLASS 2 under refinement of the existing inputs: anatomy, signed scanner/contact error, local force, fracture mechanism, biological response, cement field, implant pose/sinus and bone stiffness. Its ninth overarching example is an analytical crack-tip singularity. Among 4132 dependent canal queries, 3648 have a finite conditional numerical budget and 484 do not; physical surface certificates number zero. X31 (historical external artifact: `batch3/demos/X31/README_DEMO.md`) similarly leaves physical nerve clearance and remaining dentin UNKNOWN: total hard tissue cannot substitute for dentin without a local material observation.

Clinical durability remains outside the demonstrated scope. X32's external historical cohort records only 4 framework fractures among 230 failures; material and fracture-origin matching could reduce that mechanism overlap to zero. This is POPULATION failure-family coverage, not predictive accuracy or a modern zirconia risk estimate. Counts exclude 125/2706 incomplete planning sites and 126/148 cement cells outside the marginal comparison; omitted cases are not zero effects.

Independent batch3 (historical external artifact: `../LANE_XREVIEW_BATCH3/RESULTS.md`), restorative (historical external artifact: `../PROOF_LANE_XREVIEW_RESTORATIVE/RESULTS.md`) and export (historical external artifact: `reviews/XREV_batch7_pkg/RESULTS.md`) reviews support these bounded numbers and document rejected fault injections. Several controls required correction. The old X1 rejection remains historical; X1b has a separate protocol and scorer. Equally informed conventional calculations reproduce the stated numerical controls; no general algorithm advantage is established.

The [measurement plan](MEASUREMENT_PLAN.md) starts with registered fit/film metrology, then the frozen thickness/angle fracture contrast.
