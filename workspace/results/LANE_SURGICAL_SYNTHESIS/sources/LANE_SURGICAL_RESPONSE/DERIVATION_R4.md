## The new chemistry operator after the previous FAIL/TIE

States D,H,A are measured in aldehyde/crosslink moles per reference mol of collagen. D is available divalent precursor units; H is HP; A collects other consumed aldehyde equivalents. For HP yield η:

`Ddot=-v`, `Hdot=η*v/2`, `Adot=(1−η)*v`.

Internal flux cancels in `D+2H+A`. Cmass does not change from these reactions. For `v=kD`, D=D0 exp(−kΔt); for `v=αD²`, D=D0/(1+αD0Δt). H=H0+η(D0−D)/2. The difference between the laws cannot be identified from only two assay time points: both hit the external endpoint values exactly and give different skin forecasts. Apparent rates are also affected by undeveloped mark classes; these are closures, not elementary chemical constants.

Checkpoint D,H,A and rate/yield/reference suffice for the future in the **isolated autonomous closure**, cost O(1) per time/query. R3's global O2/cell state is unchanged. The strongest conventional reaction/renewal model has the same state and formula, thus no demonstration gain. Causal load/O2 feedback, spatial sample sampling and richer joint distribution require additional states/closures.
