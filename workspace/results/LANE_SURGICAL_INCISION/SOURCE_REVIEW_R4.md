## Verklig skillnad mellan observationsoperatorer

Barnett uses `∫(P−P_repeat)dl / ∫a dl` on a steady interval after the first puncture peak and before relaxation. A second passage through the SAME hole is assumed to have the same spreading/friction but no new fracture. This is a material closure that can fail with conditioning, changed contact, relaxation or new microfracture. Group means from Fig9 are therefore **effective** assay toughness under the closure, not microscopic gamma. All new Owen punctures are at a NEW site; needle reuse is tool history and must not be confused with Barnett's same-hole repeat.

Fig12's ink-marked crack length is measured optically after passage, with SD. It is no damage margin and does not certify loaded new3D crack area. Fig9 shows error bars whose statistical definition is not stated in that caption; the analysis uses them as reported plot bands and does not assign95%CI to them. Fig15/16 describe SD. No cross-variable covariance or same-sample posterior has been acquired.

Measurement separation thus exists: first-minus-repeat removes recurring spreading/friction together under the closure; phase3 attempts to isolate friction after full penetration. **Numerical per-tool friction/spreading ports remain null.** The study reports mean fractions61/18/21% in its fitted model, but these are not an exact budget per gauge or the skin's biological parameters. Spreading/contact factor f is fitted separately to total force for each training gauge; it is not an independent held-case acquisition.
