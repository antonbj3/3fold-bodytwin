# Sources and reuse, round 1

The lane was empty at start, apart from the night's run log. No WORK_STATUS, CHECKPOINT, RESULTS or NEXT_ROUND existed. It is therefore a new chain built on the specified HX parents.

Q033 is imported and run read-only. Its gap is an input ellipse, not a mechanically predicted gap. The new P1 model's area is passed to the same Darcy/evaporation flow per area. Q033's 4,2–6,0 N are decision thresholds from **dog cadavers**, which the parent's PREREG already explains; they are not human measurements or gap data. [Lackmann et al.](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10699892/)

Q036 is imported read-only for its actual state vector and nonlinearly coupled no-reflow/edema/metabolism. A mechanical damage proxy just ahead of the cut tip is passed to initial M/D and external perfusion through declared synthetic coefficients. The muscle model's rates validate no cut edge in skin. Q049's cartilage poroelasticity is retained as a parent for layer/transport thinking; its preserved FAIL for fluid load fraction motivates not transferring its material parameters to skin.

Private scripts were read without running them. Skin/pulp is a confined compression model. The collagen modules' modulus and melting temperature computations give neither fracture energy per mechanical bond nor the dermis's cohesive strength. The hemostasis and healing scripts have side effects on import; they were therefore not imported. No specified forbidden internal data directories were opened.

[Pissarenko et al. 2020](https://meyersgroup.ucsd.edu/papers/journals/Meyers%20477.pdf) measures juvenile pig skin with different tearing modes: about 20,4 kJ/m² in mode III and 30,4 kJ/m² in mode I. These are references for tearing with collagen rearrangement, not a scalpel intercept. No matched series with edge radius, cutting force, strength, gap and cell damage was found in the sources read.

[Goda et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.02021) gives an executable test in PAAm, with separate cutting, tensile and pure-shear assays. Published regression summaries are intercept 1,6 J/m² and radius slope 31 kPa; SI reports tearing toughness 2,6±0,2 J/m² and critical tensile energy density 6,973±0,5 kJ/m³. Under their wear-dominated approximation, 4Wf predicts the radius slope. We test this published relation and its transfer limit. We attribute no novelty to it. PDF and text are archived locally; figures have not been turned into invented raw points. The five computed force values in world_transfer.json are evaluations of regression lines, explicitly not measurements.

The [OMAG study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5632107/) measures projected vessel area in papillary/reticular dermis. This does not identify how many oriented vessels an incision severs, their radii or upstream pressure. The [standardized Ivy study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0006497120816978) reports a mean of 5 minutes and a two-SD interval of 2,5–10 minutes in the control group. That is a different observation from surgical bleeding. Bleeding and hemostasis time are kept UNKNOWN, not adjusted to this reference.

The other lanes' NEXT_ROUND and r1 were read: QUERY_GRAPH and COUPLED_RESPONSE warn that Green/Volterra/response performance against replay is not a gain against a same-information control, and that history/residuals must follow port composition. This is used in our strongest control contract and in the signed cross-link term in residual-Gram. HISTORY_INVERSE had no NEXT_ROUND/WORK_STATUS at the check; its present HANDOFF/checkpoint remains as a separate source. The glucose operators' numerical coefficients were not transferred to tissue.

The graph packet BT-CTX-SURG-INCISION was read; it is definition-only with numerical_dispatch_allowed=false. The detail graph's SKIN-DERMAL-STIFFNESS-COLLAGEN-COMPOSITION has, at reading, a claim about adipocyte count/fat mass, showing an actual subject/code binding gap. No scientific edge or status is changed.
