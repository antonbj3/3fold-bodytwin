# Precisions before claims, R2

The steering's 150 J/m² micro-scale number is **not a measured collagen bond cleavage energy**. Reading `../LANE_SURGICAL_BINDINGS/bindings_r1.py:30` and `MECHANICAL_RESULTS_R1.json` gives a nominal short-fibril pullout energy of **12 J/m²**, and a **150 J/m² strength-limited upper envelope** `phi*sigma_f*l/2`, with synthetic `phi=.3`, `sigma_f=100 MPa`, `l=10 µm`. The 15 kJ/m² fiber value is another explicitly synthetic pullout calculation at `l=5 mm`, `r=5 µm`, `tau=10 kPa`. Neither value is a jointly calibrated molecular-to-skin parameter. We preserve the nominated 150/15000 preregistered candidate and its failures; 150 is used as an optimistic scenario, not quietly relabelled as a derived intrinsic cleavage prediction.

Pissarenko's exact source is Table **1**, not the provisional Table 2 named in PREREG_R2. The preregistered 20600/30380 J/m² targets did not change. Its microscopy shows bridging, delamination and eventual fiber rupture during tearing. It does not support an exclusive intact-fiber-pullout tear mechanism. The paper's rounded abstract 20.4/30.4 differs from the table; the frozen table means remain the targets.

The initial layered run used one oblique fiber family in the upper half. Its mirror extension represents +/- families in different halves, rather than the same mirrored distribution in each layer. The final version uses two equally weighted +/- families in each layer, preserving the half-domain symmetry. Initial results and code are retained. The initial reported `projected_crack_length_m` was the quadrature weight of free seam nodes; physical crack length is the prescribed endpoint separation and is used in final force derivatives. Most increments cancel the difference, but the first release-force increment does not. Initial force values are superseded by the final-version runs, without overwriting their raw files.

Smith's abstract verifies a 12 µm scalpel tip radius but the thesis PDF could not be retrieved (old host DNS, repository 403, redirected destination 404). Its numerical energies remain unknown. `literature/r2/barnett_2016.pdf` is a preserved failed acquisition response (HTML, **not a PDF**) and is never parsed as evidence. Barnett's author's university page and indexed primary text verify double insertion and average force fractions; OCR equations and unverified numerical toughness values are excluded from calculation.

The coating study Chu2019 measures force in **pig muscle**, oil-drop sliding on kitchen blades, and skin graft morphology/healing separately. Its 51%/80% force reductions are not skin cutting energies. The 1900g/700g knife thresholds in PMID32162009 have different damage endpoints (full transection versus into-or-through dermis), unknown edge radii and missing work/area. Unit conversion is permitted; neither energy inference nor a matched sharpness gain is supported.
