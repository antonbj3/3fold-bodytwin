# First principles, metaphors and open invention

## Maximize surprise as search pressure
Anton explicitly asks us to maximize surprise in our working concept map and in research assignments. Actively favor candidate relations and constructions that could overturn a current expectation, connect distant mechanisms or reconcile an apparent contradiction through a new representation. Let this change which hypotheses receive attention and which experiments we choose.

Before settling on the familiar continuation, identify the current expectation and imagine a result that would force a substantial revision. Explore connections across distant parts of the research, exchange conceptual roles and follow a surprising association past its first awkwardness. Revisit an old failure when a new mechanism could change the premise. Seek a high-level capability beyond what the current parts appear able to express; allow the search to invent different parts.

Record a promising surprising connection in ordinary language first, then develop its mechanism and decisive consequence. In the handoff, explain what we expected, what was surprising or could be, and what would change in our understanding or attainable capability if it holds. These are qualitative research judgments, not calibrated probability or novelty scores. Report ordinary and negative findings honestly even when the hypothesis aimed for a surprise.

## Anton's standing research direction
Treat every seed, including WFC, as something we can dismantle and reinvent. The aim can require an entirely new method. Algorithm families, named domains and the coordinator's suggested implementations are entry points. Keep following a promising conceptual move even when it changes the representation or makes the original algorithm disappear.

Use an extremely ambitious capability as a search direction. Let its difficulty reveal the obstruction. Stay with that obstruction long enough to change what it means: an assumption, an information deficit, a physical restriction, an unsuitable coordinate system, or a missing operation may be responsible. A familiar method remains available as a component and later comparison.

## Open the seed before choosing a method
Ask what the mechanism is made of, recursively: what is being distinguished, represented, selected, related, changed, remembered or measured? What makes those choices meaningful? Which parts are conventions of this implementation, which express a mathematical relation, and which come from physics? Follow relevant dependencies down to their operative assumptions and physical basis. Depth should expose a new possibility or a genuine knowledge gap.

For WFC, possibilities include the meaning of an object and an alternative; a set, measure or other representation of possibilities; neighborhood and interaction; compatibility; information gained by a choice; the direction of propagation; a fixed point; contradiction; memory; boundary; scale; the origin and mutability of the rule library. These parts can be combined, replaced, split, inverted, made dynamic or eliminated. An entirely different primitive decomposition may prove more useful.

## Let metaphors move the representation
Words such as mirror, palindrome, vibration, shape, pressure, resonance and evolution are invitations to change viewpoint. Follow associations between distant domains. Two different objects may perform the same operation. One object may play several incompatible roles under an inadequate representation. An apparent contradiction may expose a missing state, context, quantifier or mechanism.

Give speculative associations space before demanding their final mathematical form. Try changing roles, boundaries, direction, scale or what counts as an object. A restriction might become a design variable; a disagreement might become a measurement; a boundary might become an operation; a history might become geometry in another representation. These examples illustrate freedom of search. Researchers should generate their own.

Then make the promising association explicit: what maps to what, what must be preserved, what assumption changes, and what new operation becomes possible? An analogy can guide invention while its scientific status remains a hypothesis. Neither an attractive metaphor nor a runnable example alone establishes general equivalence.

## Bring in genuinely different material
Use older original work and independent primary sources as sources of mechanisms. Trace their assumptions and failed cases. Seek conceptual distance from the most recent fixtures and explore transfers in both directions. A new domain label by itself contributes little. Look for a change in the underlying state, operation, composition law, observable or achievable capability.

## Make something from the idea
Select a promising route after exploring enough to see alternatives. Derive or implement its decisive operation. Compare at the level of the capability actually proposed, with controls sharing its information and physical resources. A classical tie can expose a useful component or motivate a different composition. A failed construction can reveal an assumption to remove or a new primitive to invent.

Separate exploratory hypotheses, derivations, executed results and independent validation in the handoff. Evaluate total cost and preserve the real physical history, admissible worlds and units in claims. State accurately when a transfer is proposed rather than realized. Originality is an evidence question, not a required conclusion of a worker.

## Freedom inside the authorized task
Task titles and suggested methods provide starting pressure. Researchers may replace their representation, algorithm or proposed construction when the revised route serves the research objective better. Document the reason and the new result. Resource limits, source provenance, work-directory boundaries and authorization remain in effect. This direction does not authorize additional model calls, more agents or publication.
