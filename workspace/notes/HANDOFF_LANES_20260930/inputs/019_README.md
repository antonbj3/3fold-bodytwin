## Architecture to test

Use the existing typed source view and engine; implement a small reader from artifacts/cards to candidate mechanisms, their declared ports, assumptions, goals, source versions and executed tests. Preserve distinct project namespaces. Represent a multi-part hypothesis as its own node with ordered/member roles and a joint test; pairwise edges alone cannot express irreducible three-way effects.

Keep several notions of proximity visible: behavior/operation, graph connectivity, subject, and compatibility of physical assumptions and ports. Seek both structural analogies across distant subjects and complementary operations that are deliberately unlike. Missing information is an acquisition target, not a maximum-distance score. Similar words are neither concept identity nor mechanistic evidence.

Record proposed, attempted, completed, refuted and independently reviewed combinations separately. Coverage is relative to the declared candidate universe and representation; no claim to exhaust all triples. Record corpus snapshots, draw probabilities, cost, failures and parent lineage. The candidate view must not convert speculative associations to scientific evidence.

## Six parallel free-model investigations

1. **Mechanism geometry:** extend existing signatures with operator/port/state structure; find useful distant transfers and deceptive near-neighbors.
2. **Graph as an adaptive field:** use resistance, uncertainty, interface refinement and stress localization to seek neglected combinations; ask what field/contact algorithms can teach the graph and vice versa.
3. **Irreducible combinations:** WFC/CSP and symbolic operator effects as constituents; invent a search that detects a useful triple or larger combination missed by pairwise affinity.
4. **Contradictions as construction sites:** exploit failed transfers and counterexamples to propose a new operation or observation with a real capability gain.
5. **Learning how to search:** learn from outcomes without eliminating diversity, inheriting duplicates as independent evidence or rewarding favorable summaries; compare equal-budget selection policies.
6. **Working integration:** reuse existing graph contracts and APIs to produce a source-bound read-only prototype over the supplied corpus, then demonstrate actual source retrieval and an executable cross-domain test.

All receive the existing engine code, tests, compact full-corpus manifest, frozen cards and older mechanism packets. No paid wave, source-repo edits, pin updates, publishing or native evidence promotion.

## What counts as improvement

Compare current recent-parent selection, uniform stratified selection, existing engine selection and any proposed extension using the same candidate pool, execution budget, model, information and validation rules. Separate outcomes on actual research packets from planted fixtures. Historical outcomes have selection and review bias; offline replay cannot invent unobserved counterfactual outcomes. Primary outcomes: valid new capability or falsifying mechanism, independently reproduced connection, cost per useful result, and breadth retained across older families. Counts of words, reports or graph edges do not establish a gain.

Self-improvement is a testable loop: the selector is itself a versioned mechanism with assumptions, observations, failures and competing variants. Freeze the evaluator before allowing feedback to influence selection; retain a held-out/control slice. An evaluator that rewards its own proposals is a failure mode to test.
