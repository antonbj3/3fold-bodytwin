# Ambitious goals as a search method

Anton, 2026-09-27. Applies to proof_lane, Sol, The swarm and free_worker. Source: the original graph README, `projects/graph_workspace/pub/README.md`, initial public commit `0d836fa` (2026-09-13), especially The unlock graph, Densification and Big goals as coverage probes. This describes how to search; it does not certify the README's individual scientific claims.

**Aim at a consequential capability.** Use an extremely ambitious goal to expose missing knowledge and representations: what useful operation would become possible if the present restriction disappeared? Work backward from that capability. A major gain is a search aspiration, never an instruction to report a positive result. A small experiment should illuminate a large goal.

**Open the load-bearing obstacle.** Express the goal as dependencies and identify the requirement preventing progress. Recursively unpack it into constituents, interactions, equations, representations and assumptions, following FIRST_PRINCIPLES.md. Separate physical, information, mathematical, numerical and implementation limits. Preserve unknown leaves and the argument for stopping the decomposition. Challenge whether the chosen variables, outputs or boundaries created an avoidable limit.

**Stay at disagreement.** A failed attempt narrows that attempt's validity; it need not close the parent goal. Record the newly exposed requirement and propose genuinely different ways to meet it. Resolve conflicting results by inspecting their assumptions, mechanisms and observables. Rotate the whole problem through useful alternatives: inverse/forward, primal/dual, state/observable, spatial/temporal, local/global, geometry/topology, representation and acquisition. These are prompts, not a mandatory checklist or a limit on creativity. An impossibility proof must say which assumptions would have to change.

**Densify deliberately.** Look at the boundary between understood and missing mechanisms. Force connections among two to four supplied results, including earlier waves and failed branches. Prefer mechanisms that are structurally compatible even when their application domains are far apart. Write the shared operator or physical relation, quantities/units, assumptions, predicted benefit and counterexample. Names or graph proximity alone establish no transfer. Seek an observation that distinguishes opposing explanations rather than repeating the same evidence.

**Choose by what the result unlocks.** Rank next experiments by the important goals they could unlock, their information value and complete cost. Explain the estimate without invented precision. Select distinct branches with actual consumers; do not manufacture near-duplicate jobs to fill quotas. Give strong conventional controls the same information, accuracy, design freedom and cost accounting. Build a discriminating construction early and carry useful partial mechanisms forward.

**Leave branches that another worker can use.** For each explored seed record its parent/source, actual result, binding requirement, next mechanism, decisive test and downstream goal. Emit a compact SEED_EXPANSIONS.json (or these fields in an existing structured output). Map it to follow-up proposal IDs, already completed work, or an explicit prerequisite/refutation/deferment. Proposals use the existing SWARM_QUEUE_ADD.json or FOLLOWUPS.json schema. Do not rerun an unchanged failed approach. A queue entry or graph edge is not scientific acceptance.

**Use the available graph honestly.** BodyTwin and dental workers receive selected graph packets; dispatch and feedback are handled by their existing controller. Keep target/source IDs in results and report missing dependencies for review. Field/proof_lane use linked result packets and queues as well; do not claim that they are a complete shared knowledge graph. Do not edit canonical graphs or mark your own findings validated.

For a bounded The swarm/free_worker job, implement one valuable step in this chain and leave explicit next branches. For a planner, expand and rank several distinct steps. For a Sol review, identify which evidence changes the next construction. Existing resource limits and task scope still apply; this message authorizes no extra paid agents.

# Stay at the stress point

Anton, 2026-09-27: waves must be aggressive, forward-moving, and stay at the stress point. This is an instruction about how to search. A large claimed gain is never a completion requirement.

Start from the ambitious capability and the obstacle that prevents it. Force structural connections among earlier and recent results: operators, state variables, geometry, information, physical interactions, representations and certificates. Merely transplanting a known method into another example does not complete a breakthrough investigation.

For constructive research, follow this loop within the authorized runtime:

1. Name the load-bearing obstacle and the useful capability unlocked by removing it. Reconstruct the relevant supplied mechanisms from equations and code. Break each down until its assumptions and remaining unknowns are explicit.
2. Build an actual combination. State which operation from each parent changes the construction and why their interaction could remove the obstacle. Include a control that removes each added mechanism; juxtaposed modules alone do not establish a combination gain.
3. Execute a discriminating test early. Keep the accuracy, information, design freedom, output contract and complete costs equal for strong controls.
4. At a failure or tie, stay with the parent goal. Locate the cause: missing information, physical law, branch explosion, representation cost, primitive output size, error accumulation or another specific obstruction. Use that diagnosis to change a consequential assumption or operation. Inverse/forward, state/observable, spatial/temporal, primal/dual, exact/controlled approximation, local/global and another domain's mechanism are available directions, not an exhaustive list.
5. Execute the most promising changed construction before ending when the budget permits. Do not make three cosmetic variants to satisfy a quota. If a proof rules out the current contract, find which changed contract would recover value and test its smallest useful instance. Preserve the obstruction and every failed comparison.
6. Carry the unresolved stress point into an executable successor. A bounded worker may finish, but its parent search remains open with a specific next operation or missing prerequisite. A resource interruption does not mean that the scientific idea failed.

Save compact ATTEMPTS.json entries: parent artifact/seed IDs, hypothesis, changed operation, mathematical compatibility, experiment and evidence path, strongest comparator, outcome, diagnosed obstacle, next operation. RESULTS.md must distinguish a measured application gain, a useful capability, a new conjecture, a proved statement under assumptions, and an unresolved question. Equal performance by an equally informed conventional method is reported honestly; investigate whether the combined capability or a subsequent representation change still removes a consequential restriction.

For planners: choose experiments whose interaction can unlock the ambitious capability. Read failures as branch points. Do not spend the round only cataloguing, adding adapters, repeating validation, or proposing more generic planners. Retain original seed IDs across waves. Reuse existing equivalent jobs, and explain the new delta before adding a successor. Explicit audit jobs retain their narrow independent checking role. Constructive work gets first scheduling priority; the audit queue remains available before scientific acceptance.

Existing runtime, memory, source boundaries and model permissions remain in force. Automatic work uses only the configured free models. No additional Sol, proof_lane or coordinator wave is authorized.

# Goals that generate the stress points

These are desired research capabilities beyond the current delivered system, not claims that they are possible under every input or physical law. Every descendant must name its parent goal, derive the conflict it attacks, and explain how its small experiment bears on that conflict. Preserve the full goal after partial success.

## CONTACT — inverse design of an entire evolving contact scene

Interactively change geometry, materials, loading, assembly and objectives in a large scene with friction, irreversible history and changing contact topology; obtain a feasible design and its full path constraints without repeatedly solving the entire scene or replaying its past. Aim for update cost tied to the decision-relevant change rather than total scene and history size.

Required together: global equilibrium, local pressure/force constraints, irreversible memory, changed contact branches, inverse queries, and useful update cost. Derive the conflict between global feedback and local symbolic reuse. Combine event algebra, inverse guards, Schur ports, material-labelled history, source uncertainty and geometry. A two-pad rank-one update is an experimental instrument; it does not replace the scene goal.

## ADAPTIVE_FIELD — one editable field with sufficient resolution for any requested physical question

Represent coupled geometry, material state and history so that cuts, deformations, thin layers, new loads and different observation scales can be queried and optimized without globally expanding space × time × state. Resolve what the output requires, including interactions across regions, and retain a defensible output error budget.

Required together: nonlocal physical coupling, arbitrary spatial edits/queries, history dependence, fine local structure, conservation and affordable adaptive work. Derive the conflict between shared event compression and propagation of influence. Independent per-cell toy sums do not satisfy this goal. First attack their independence assumption with a well-posed coupled model, then explore dual observables, compressed response, influence bounds and symbolic event propagation. A time-grid sweep alone does not cross this boundary.

## GRAPH_INVERSE — a graph that assembles the cheapest sufficient scientific computation for a new question

Given a previously unseen question spanning geometry, mechanics, physiology, measurements and uncertainty, assemble and refine only the observations and computations needed to answer it reliably. Retain shared causes and state changes, carry unresolved physical assumptions, and reuse the result for different future questions.

Required together: relevant information without full hidden-state recovery, coupled physical consequences, justified uncertainty, automatic operation composition, and a reduction in total acquisition and reasoning work. Derive where decision equivalence breaks under a new goal, action or source law. Combine goal pullbacks, joint information state, operator proofs and value of information. An optimal order on three fixed tests is an instrument, not completion of this goal.

## MULTIPHYSICS — joint generative design across geometry, process, material and physiology

Search geometry, composition, manufacturing path and operating schedule together under interacting mechanical, thermal, transport, binding and biological requirements across time and spatial scales. Reuse expensive knowledge so that adding an objective or changing a design need not restart the full coupled computation.

Required together: feedback among processes, local transients, multiple competing objectives, latent material state, physical inventories, uncertainty and inverse design. Derive the incompatibility between reusable fixed operators and temperature-/state-dependent laws. Combine symbolic cost/guard records, response-aware resolution, inverse feasible sets and mechanistic closures. Separate unknown empirical constitutive data from a mathematical barrier. A finite parameter sweep is a first instrument, not the goal.

## RSI_OPERATORS — discoveries improve the machinery of subsequent discovery

Acquire verified operations and representations that materially change the ability to discover the next operations, repeatedly across new task families and domains, rather than merely memoizing answers. Aim to cross the current combinatorial search, representation growth and verification-cost boundaries while retaining executable outputs and charging all discovery costs.

Required together: reusable semantics, changed goals/actions, affordable primitive output and checking, useful generalization and a mechanism that improves future search. Derive the tension between expressivity and closure size. Combine implicit observable algebra, proof DAGs, event/guard/cost composition, inverse search and learned acquisition. The 256 active bits describe state dimension; they do not establish this capability or numerical precision. No finite example establishes general RSI.

## MOTION_RL — acquire transferable physical skills without relearning each body and scene

Learn or construct contact-rich behavior that transfers across changed morphology, geometry, materials, constraints and tasks, while retaining all intermediate physical constraints and learning from incomplete observations. Aim to reuse a small amount of new evidence instead of repeating full simulation and search for each new setting.

Required together: branch-changing contact, irreversible state, partial observation, new goals, useful transfer and constraint preservation. Derive where a policy or macro action ceases to have the same semantics. Combine symbolic physical operators, goal-conditioned observables, graph acquisition and adaptive simulation. A fixed macro library matching a conventional options method is a clue to the next obstruction, not fulfillment of this goal.
