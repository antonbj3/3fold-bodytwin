## The proposal from the conversation

One concrete track is to **let movement help us determine the geometry**:

1. Construct different anatomical models that fit the same nine landmarks but predict different moment arms.
2. Identify which movement or extra measurement best distinguishes the models.
3. Fit joint centres, muscle attachments and muscle paths jointly to the observations. The field engine can be tested as a representation of how muscle paths run around bones and other tissues.
4. Test predictions on movements and individuals not used for fitting, against a baseline that receives the same information.

**The hypothesis is that the interaction of form and function contains information the landmarks alone lack.** The first experiment must show whether that information can actually be recovered. It gives us a concrete mechanism to try to build an advantage on.
