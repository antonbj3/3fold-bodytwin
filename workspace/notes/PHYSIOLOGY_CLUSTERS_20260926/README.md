# BodyTwin: mitochondrial stress and immunity

Goal: reconstruct stress energetics, immune protection, cancer response and healthy-organ injury from explicit interacting components, then determine what measurements and resolution each question needs.

This package adds 16 mitochondria/stress questions, 24 immunity questions and 4 cross-cluster investigations. Each is decomposed into state, relation, observation and test nodes. [Graph proposal](CLUSTER_GRAPH_PROPOSAL.json) is a research specification; native graph admission remains separate.

- [Mitochondria and stress](MITOSTRESS.md)
- [Immunity, underactivity, overactivity and cancer-treatment effects](IMMUNITY.md)
- [Cross-cluster experiments](CROSS.md)
- [Methods and substances](METHODS_AND_PERTURBATIONS.md)
- [First-principles rule](FIRST_PRINCIPLES.md)
- [Existing code and negative evidence](EXISTING_WORK.md), [inventory](CODE_INVENTORY.json)
- [Source register](SOURCES.md), [claim audit](CLAIM_AUDIT.md)
- [Concrete queued job definitions](JOBS.json)

The curated working graph previously had no dedicated actions for these clusters. Two explicit definition/review targets are added as local research actions. They are linked to relevant native anchors as context only; neither a femur error-budget result nor an imported OPEN node is evidence for immune physiology. Numerical experiment dispatch stays closed until required inputs and calibration are bound. Conditional synthetic probes are permitted to test mathematical mechanisms.

All new tasks must preserve dimensions, compartments, positive inventories, charge/energy/mass accounting where applicable, cell-type and species distinctions, and independent observations. Detailed chemistry is expanded when it controls the answer; missing rate constants, constitutive closures and observation maps remain visible. Discovery is judged by a changed prediction or a discriminating test, not by node count.
