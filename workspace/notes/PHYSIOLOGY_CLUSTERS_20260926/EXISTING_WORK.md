# Existing work and negative evidence to preserve

Source-only snapshots are included. Several native scripts execute simulations and write absolute paths on import. Never import/run them unchanged: inspect with AST, isolate required functions and constants in this job directory, list dependencies, preserve provenance, and compare the extracted operator with its source. Native docstring labels such as CERTIFIED are historical author claims, not new admission.

Relevant native anchors remain OPEN: MODEL-MITOCHONDRIAL-BIOENERGETICS, MODEL-HPA-CORTISOL-ULTRADIAN, MODEL-PD1-TCELL-EXHAUSTION, MODEL-TUMOR-IMMUNE-CHECKPOINT, MODEL-TUMOR-IMMUNE-DYNAMICS, MODEL-COMPLEMENT-AMPLIFICATION, MODEL-MACROPHAGE-POLARIZATION and GOAL-CANCER-IMMUNE-WARGAME-TWIN. Exact native records are retained locally, not shipped as whole graphs.

- Native PD1/exhaustion graph records already flag a mismatch between numerical headline claims and the actual T-cell script. Do not inherit the ratios as reproduced measurements.
- Native checkpoint response model explicitly lacks a calibrated PK/dose axis; apparent responder fractions can be changed by choosing dose. Population eligibility statistics cannot calibrate a mechanistic dose axis.
- Native tumor dynamics record contains weak held-out performance and qualitative-only anchors. Different species and observables are not numerically interchangeable.
- HPA has competing delay descriptions and existing graph contradiction nodes. BT-HX-Q023 onset lag failed its frozen gate. Do not retune and relabel the same test as external validation.
- BT-HX-Q077 geometry gave only a 1.0195 flux ratio against its 1.10 gate; transport time and throughput are distinct outputs. Segmentation/contact-area uncertainty in BT-HX3-MITO-CONTACT can inflate the apparent gain.
- Q082 preserves amount and charge under moving volumes. Q083 fusion/fission includes illustrative response closures. Copies are synthetic model sources with their original reports, not calibrated physiology.
- Macrophage polarization code uses a reduced M1/M2 framing and disclosed context dependence; real tissue states require richer representations.
- Complement already has amplification/bistability and dose-blind Boolean-gate critiques. Compare against those, not a newly invented weak baseline.
