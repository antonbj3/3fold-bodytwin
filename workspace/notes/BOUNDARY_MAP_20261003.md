### Warning about unit matching (applies to all of section 3)

2. **`s` and `min` are mostly cost, not observable** — 46 of the 86 only matched on wall clock.
3. **`phosphor` → `phosphorylation`** (N3 above).
The final number below (40) is after all three corrections.
