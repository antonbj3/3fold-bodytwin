## 1. The eye model was compared to real surgical outcomes for the first time

Source: a published dataset of post-operative refraction, DOI `10.6084/m9.figshare.22736687.v1`,
20 held-out rows. Nothing was fitted to them.

| treatment | bias | MAE | RMSE | max error | within 0.25 D | within 0.50 D |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| thin lens | **−0.3865 D** | 0.5301 | 0.6591 | 1.2980 | 7/20 | 12/20 |
| thick lens | **+0.1455 D** | 0.4697 | 0.5902 | 1.4350 | 7/20 | 13/20 |
| index-convention control | +0.1643 D | 0.4754 | 0.5951 | 1.4529 | 7/20 | 13/20 |

**Not yet clinically usable, and the error is localised.** The systematic bias is a lens-model artefact:
−0.39 D with a thin lens, +0.15 D with a thick one. The index-convention control reproduces the
thick-lens figure, which rules out a unit or convention artefact.

The cornea side was separately eliminated as the missing piece. Two corneas with **identical** mean
thickness, refractive index field, hydration and surface allocation — all identity errors exactly 0.0 —
differ by **0.3056 D**, in 4 of 4 tested pairs. What is missing is the three-dimensional surface shape
field and a common coordinate registration. A clinical corneal topographer measures exactly that.

**The chain itself is load-bearing.** Halving the molecular charge inventory
(1.2195e-15 → 6.0976e-16 mol/m) moves the ionic swelling pressure by **−489.65 Pa**, the pupil-mean
thickness by 3.54 µm and the refractive power by **−0.4256 D**, with the Strehl ratio falling
0.1227 → 0.1042. A rotationally symmetric cornea of the same mean thickness gives **−1.36e-08 D**, so
the whole effect comes from non-symmetric structure. Numerics: 4.19e-13 D round-off.

**But the sign is not yet predictable.** Across twelve conditions the change ranges from +0.4658 D to
−0.8618 D; five of ten clear the 0.25 D clinical threshold and the sign flips. The span over the
surface-allocation parameter alone is 0.8724 D, which coincides to four decimals with the measured
sufficiency gap. So the effect is clinically large and its direction is undetermined until surface shape
is measured.

## 2. Laser: three conventional dose measures were shown insufficient

| measure tested | identity error | downstream difference | counterexamples |
|---|---|---|---|
| delivered energy | **0.0 J** | removed mass 0.0 against 1.255e-9 kg | — |
| surface temperature history | **0.0 K** | **0.3806 K** at target depth | — |
| CEM43 with time and peak temperature | **0.0** | **5.04 percentage points** in conditional loss | 7 |

Numerical control for the depth case: 4.33e-05 K at the finest finite volume, three orders of magnitude
below the gap, so it is physical.

Surface temperature is the quantity a surgeon can measure during the procedure, and CEM43 is the standard
clinical thermal-dose measure. Neither determines the tissue response in this chain.

A published ablation threshold of **1.15 J/cm²** was acquired, defined as the x-intercept of a mass-loss
regression with mass corrected for evaporation. Our chain sits **26.5 % above it** with a slope error of
−25.0 %; the diagnosed cause is a mismatch between the source's fluence convention and the observation
definition, not a parameter error.

## 3. Complement regulation: a threshold with a unit

As the C3b recycling fraction rises to **0.6901**, the growth rate on a self surface crosses zero
(−0.1511 → −0.0019 per minute) and MAC at 60 minutes rises **0.822 → 8.484 nM**.

Discrimination between an activator surface and a self surface is **486.1** without recycling and
**4.969** with it — a factor of **97.8** on one modelling choice. **Published biology supports the low
end** (immediate Bb dissociation supports the recycling product), so the protective margin is roughly
fivefold, not five-hundredfold.

The threshold is **not** certified as load-bearing: the source's own uncertainty on the parameter is
unmeasured, and the gate is recorded as FAIL for that reason.

## 5. Summary statistics are insufficient far more often than they are tested

Measured across our own cells and contracts, with every identity error at machine precision:

| test | result |
|---|---|
| adversarially selected contracts | **45 of 51** testable fail, 0.882 |
| random audit sample, n = 30 of frame 260 | 20 fail, 4 hold, 6 unresolved → **0.833** of testable |
| random source-native sample, n = 30 | 12 fail, 4 hold, 14 unresolved → **0.750** of testable |

Method: exact integer hypergeometric equal-tail inversion, union over every unresolved label, **no
missing-at-random assumption**.

**This is bounded, not certified.** The 95 % finite-frame intervals on the population proportion are
**[0.485, 0.958]** and [0.238, 0.958], so "fewer than half" is not excluded. The interval width is
driven by the 20 unresolved labels, not by sample size: resolving them would collapse it, another
30 samples would not.

**Our own acceptance gates carry the same defect.** Of 43 cells, **21 accept on a scalar point-or-band
test and only 7 carry any distributional statistic**. Eight have no distributional check at all. When a
distributional held-out test was built and run on those eight, **3 of 8 verdicts flipped** — they pass
their current gate and fail the distribution. The case that started this: a hemostasis cell passes its
point-in-band gate while **7.305 %** of its predictive mass never closes at all, stable at 0.069 across
time steps 0.020, 0.010 and 0.004, so structural rather than numerical.

## 6. One of our own numbers was refuted by an external measurement

Our epithelial renewal front velocity is **30.0 µm/h**. The held-out measurement is
**9.00 ± 0.465 µm/h** in control and **−0.798 ± 0.942 µm/h** during the first ten hours after Ara-C —
the real front briefly retreats. Ratio 3.33, parameter-free test, nothing fitted.

The provenance is the finding: the source states the constant was *chosen to give* 0.03 mm/h. A constant
back-solved from a desired output makes every result downstream of it circular, and our census of fitted
constants did not flag it. A census class for this pattern has been added and a sweep of all 45 cells is
running.

Census state: of 1 491 quantity families, **153 are confirmed phenomenological (10.26 %)**, at most
13.62 % if all 50 unclassifiable families fall that way. The 50 hang on one source-native reference
definition — an acquisition item, not an analysis task.

## 8. Capability added

- **An external-facit channel.** 42 searches for published measurements returned **33 partial, 5 full
  hits and 5 verified absences**, with values, units and DOI or PMID locators; 55 records are already in
  consumable form, 31 of them with a declared validity range. A verified absence is a result: it says to
  change the readout instead of waiting for data.
- **Two new cells**, taking the workspace from 43 to 45: corneal scattering and complement
  discrimination, both built from domain data that no cell had touched.
- **A measured build surface.** Of 112 physiology domains, 14 are covered by a cell and 98 are not. Of
  the 98, **45 have a literature record with a unit**, so they can be certified on day one; 49 have only
  our own older numbers; 4 have neither.
- **Boundary conditions declared for every flow cell**: all 45 classified, none unknown. A stationary end
  value cannot identify a conductance, while a time-resolved transient with known compliance can — one
  documented boundary condition failed 4 of 4 tests against this.
- **An independent finite-element solver** is installed and verified, so the interoperability question
  can be tested with an implementation that is not ours.
