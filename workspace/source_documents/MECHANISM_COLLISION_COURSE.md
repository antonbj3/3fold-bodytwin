# COLLISION COURSE — standing directive, 2026-07-25

Operator direction: *"you need to get on a collision course against what exists, not just walk in circles"*
and *"jag vill bota Alzheimers, jag vill bota paralysis, alla former av cancer"*.

This overrides the habit the tick prompt encourages. The tick's audit menu (deferred arithmetic, void-floor,
script-existence) is **maintenance**. It was right for one day. It is not the mission and must not become the
default again.

## The failure mode being corrected

The instrument got polished instead of aimed. Symptom: the cancer goal node carries in-degree 1752 and zero
computed content. Every audit pointed at it; nothing was ever computed *for* it.

A second, subtler symptom: real collisions with clinical practice were produced and then **reported as audit
findings rather than as claims about the world**. That is how a collision becomes a circle.

## What counts as on-course

A cell earns its place only if it states something that **could be wrong in public** — a claim that contradicts
what is currently done, in terms a clinician could argue with. Not "cell A and cell B disagree". Something
closer to:

- Gleason GG3 and GG4 do not separate on the outcome they exist to predict (z=0.57, p=0.57).
- FPG ≥ 126 was calibrated to retinopathy prevalence and never to any beta-cell measurement.
- Simultaneous dual-lobe biopsy splits cirrhosis-vs-F3 in 14.5 % of the same patients at the same instant.
- Cognitive reserve buys time and cannot bend the post-onset trajectory.

Each of those is a target someone could shoot at. That is the bar.

## The three targets

**Paralysis.** Corticospinal axons have no renewal node, so "wait for turnover" is excluded by construction and
only *edit the programme*, *replace the source*, or *bypass the node* remain. Bypass was found in another organ
today (activity routing around a defective TBC1D4 node via a parallel pathway). Quantify the neural analogue:
which descending pathways are recruitable, and how many spared axons are actually required? The counted-floor
question, on the one system where the answer changes what is attempted.

**Alzheimer's.** Neurons do not renew — but microglia and astrocytes do. So ask the question the re-inscription
geometry actually poses: which brain nodes *have* a renewal node, such that a defect there can wash out at
turnover rate, unlike the neuron? And note the harder half: washout requires **clearance**, which tau and
lipofuscin do not undergo. That is structural, not incidental.

**Cancer.** Start from identifiability, not biology-first. What can current staging provably not distinguish,
and which observable of a *different symmetry class* breaks that collision? A SUM-type readout is blind to
which unit is which; a ratio is blind to a common scale. Name the measurement that would resolve it.

## Method, unchanged

The discipline stays exactly as it is. Symmetric QC, noise floors before any null is called reserve, a
rejection carrying the same evidence burden as a confirmation, honest negatives folded as findings, corrections
written back into the cell they were found for. **Bolder aim, same rigour.** Speculation is licensed;
sloppiness is not. Label speculation as speculation and give it a falsification surface.

## Pace

Weekly is the only wind-down gate (≥98 %). A high 5-hour percentage means conserve and keep folding, not stop.
Spawn wide the moment the window resets.
