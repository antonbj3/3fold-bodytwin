## 1. THE META-STRATEGY (the load-bearing idea — this is not a topic, it's a method)

(comparative improvement ASML in quality/yield/flexibility, shrink it to a bedroom machine — aim absurdly past the real target
so you overshoot it). Cancer is the **same knob pointed at the ADVERSARY**: model a cancer *more* aggressive,
*more* concealing, *more* immune-evasive than any real one, then build the immune/treatment strategy that beats
**that** inflated monster — so against the real (weaker) cancer you are over-equipped. Overshoot the **threat
model**, not just the goal. An **adversarial two-agent simulation**: cancer vs the immune system, cancer attacked
from **multiple decorrelated angles + modalities**, its hardness **inflated without bound** (worst-case /
adversarial-robustness design).

**Why it works (the real point):** the inflated / unbeatable adversary is a **search-control device**. If the
opponent is allowed to be beatable, the cert-frontier *converges* and node-expansion *stops* — the coordinator's
premature-convergence failure mode ([[my-stress-avoidance-is-build-as-av]], reach-for-a-confident-close). By
structurally forbidding "victory," the search **never terminates**: perpetual ongoing search, perpetual node
expansion. *"A search algorithm that never stops searching because you don't let it."*

**It is natively an anchor-graph:** "must be attacked from multiple modalities" == **decorrelated
over-determination**; every (immune weapon × cancer evasion) pair is a cell to corner. The war-game frame IS the
