# the proof lane: which shape modes carry the 0,306 D that lumped measures do not explain?

## What is measured, from our own chain

Four pairs of corneas with **identical** mean thickness, identical index field, identical hydration AND
identical surface allocation still differ in refractive power by **0,3056 D**, in 4 of 4 pairs, with
identity error at machine precision. The rotationally symmetric control case with the same mean thickness
gives **−1,36e-08 D**, thus zero. The entire effect lies in non-symmetric structure.

The range of refractive-power change over the surface-allocation parameter alone is **0,8724 D**, and the same range measured
as a sufficiency gap is 0,87239 D — the two coincide to four decimal places.

Available ground truth in the chain: 20 patients for lens power and 69 for cylinder and axis. Our chain gives
on the 69 eyes magnitude error 0,2808 D and vector error 0,4985 D with measured posterior cornea, against
0,6209/0,8923 with population estimation at one orientation and 0,3636/0,6051 at the other.

## The question

Lumped measures do not determine the outcome. A spatial field is required. But WHICH field, and which modes in it?

1. **Formulate the smallest field parameterisation that can carry 0,3056 D.** Zernike modes or another basis —
   but state which modes, why those, and how many are needed. A basis that needs twenty modes
   to explain one number is no explanation.

2. **Show why rotational symmetry gives exactly zero.** It is a strong condition and must follow from
   a symmetry property, not from a numerical coincidence. Derive it.

3. **Connect the modes to the surface-allocation range 0,8724 D.** The fact that the range over one parameter and
   the sufficiency gap coincide to four decimal places says something about how the parameter acts. What?

4. **State which measurement would decide the matter** on the 69 eyes we already have, in terms of what a
   topography or tomography actually delivers. Not "measure the shape" but which coefficients, with
   what resolution, and how many eyes are needed to distinguish the modes.

## Requirements

- Control: the lumped reading used today. Every mode explanation is calculated against it.
- Falsifier written out before each derivation: which outcome rejects the proposed basis.
- State what you CANNOT determine without shape data. A boundary on the claim is a result.
- Unit and plane in every field name. Lens and spectacle planes differ by a factor 0,6823 in our chain
  and magnitude versus vector by 1,775; the two have already been confused here.
- Precision: numbers that carry four significant digits must be written with four. One of our network numbers turned out to be
  exactly 3397/1530 and was cited with fifteen digits.
- PENDING_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW. No clinical claim.
