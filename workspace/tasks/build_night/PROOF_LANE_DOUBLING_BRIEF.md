## The question

A geometric doubling 1-2-4-8 over four cases is not a coincidence. And the sign reversal
occurs **between 2× and 4×**, thus between 0,611 D and 1,223 D, not at the smallest or largest.

1. **Why is the gap doubled?** Which operation in the chain from surface form to breaking force can give exact
   factor two per step over four cases? State it, and state what must be true if applicable
   construction for the duplication to occur. Are the cases constructed as a halving or
   doubling series in any parameter — and if so, in which?

2. **Why does the sign reverse right there?** A zero threshold crossed between 0,611 and 1,223 D defines
   an interval. What is in that range? Is the threshold clinical (0,25 D is mentioned in the net as
   clinical threshold) or is it a characteristic of the operator? If the gap doubles monotonically and the sign
   turns at a certain value, does the turning point follow the doubling or is it independent?

3. **Are the four cases independent observations or a series?** It all depends on what they can carry.
   Four independent pairs are four measurements. A doubling series is **en** measurement in four scales, and then
   is n = 1 and not 4. Determine it from the structure.

4. **What does the rotation control say 4,467e−07 D?** It is 1,5 millionths of the smallest gap. If
   rotational symmetry gives practically zero while non-symmetric structure gives 0,306 D and up,
   what is the exact symmetry property that causes it? Derive it, and state whether it is one
   characteristic of the operator or of the reading.
