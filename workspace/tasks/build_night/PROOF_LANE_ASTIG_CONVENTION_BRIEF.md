# the proof lane: is 0,1840 D astigmatic vector distance the same as our convention sensitivity?

## Two verified findings that may be the same finding

**Finding one, from the shape-mode round.** The mean power gap of 0,305639112 D is carried by radial common bending,
and radial bending alone reproduces it to 1,574e−10 D. **Pure astigmatic bending gives practically
zero mean gap but 0,1840 D in cylinder vector distance.** Rotational symmetry forces P = qI, so
astigmatism disappears while mean power q is free — I have verified the commutation myself.

**Finding two, from the toric chain.** The gain from measuring the posterior cornea depends on
the population constant’s orientation:

| arm | magnitude error (D) | vector error (D) |
|---|---|---|
| measured posterior | 0,2808 | 0,4985 |
| population at 90° | 0,6209 | 0,8923 |
| population at 0° | 0,3636 | 0,6051 |

The gain is thus **0,3401 D at 90° and 0,0828 D at 0°** in magnitude, and 0,3938 versus 0,1066 in
vector. The constant is −0,30 D and the two orientations give J0 = −0,15 and +0,15 respectively, i.e.
the same magnitude with reversed sign. Vector error is 1,775× magnitude error for the measured arm.

## The question

0,1840 D is astigmatic vector distance from a pure bending mode. The difference between our two
population arms is a change of sign on J0 with magnitude 0,15 D, giving a vector distance of
0,30 D between the two constants.

1. **Are the two numbers the same quantity?** 0,1840 D from the mode versus 0,30 D between the constants. If both
   are cylinder vector distances in the same plane and convention, how do they relate? Calculate it,
   and say whether 0,1840 lies inside or outside the interval spanned by the constant’s sign choice.

2. **Can convention sensitivity be explained by the mode?** If the posterior cornea’s astigmatism
   actually lies in a bending mode with vector distance 0,1840 D, and our constant sets it
   to 0,30 D in one of two directions, is one direction then closer to the mode than the other? This
   would decide the orientation question without a quoted nomogram, which is what currently blocks
   the entire headline.

3. **Which of the two orientation choices is compatible with radial common bending?** The mode carries
   the mean power. If the posterior surface bends with the anterior one, what sign does its J0 then get?

4. **Falsifier for the whole connection.** If the two numbers are not the same quantity, say exactly which
   quantity each is and why they cannot be compared. A limitation is a result, and that
   limitation is worth as much as a connection.
