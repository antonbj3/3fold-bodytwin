Krevolin JL, Pandy MG, Pearce JC. *Moment arm of the patellar tendon in the human knee.* J Biomech. 2004;37(5):785–788. PMID 15047009, DOI 10.1016/j.jbiomech.2003.09.010. Official abstract: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15047009/ . It reports six fresh cadaver knees, moment arms around the tibia–femur finite screw axis, maximum near 45 degrees of flexion, peak range 4–6 cm. The abstract provides no per-angle individual numeric series; do not invent one. Distinguish this measurement definition from generic OpenSim model curves.
