# CLOUD-W6-KNEE-MEASURE — measurement design to beat the empirical knee-force null

Only public sources and original mathematics/code. No restricted model data/TLEM/the collaborator/LHDL or private A matrices. No subagents, push, publishing, email or paid usage. Bounded 20 minutes. Return RESULTS.md, results.json and small runnable code between =====FILE markers, total under 200 KB.

BodyTwin's four-person Grand Challenge evaluation has 113 trials, 12,096 frames. The empirical N1g null median subject-balanced RMSE is 0.378 BW; a muscle/contact formulation H1 is 0.417 BW. A second empirical null improved pooled median to 0.367 BW but won only 2/4 leave-one-person-out rotations. A prior force-feasibility model had one correlated source, weak upper bounds and worsened JW early stance. Separately, the gc1 eKnee `Sensor1` channel correlates with synchronized Biodex torque (r=0.884 at zero lag), but the four channels lack documented physical axes, signs and calibration matrix. Do not label the channel norm as measured tibiofemoral compression. See bundled CONTEXT.md for precise provenance.

Design a *different angle* from Field's gauge-theorem BODYGRAPH: a data acquisition and model-selection protocol that could decide whether an individual physics model adds force information beyond N1g. Derive which independently measured variable(s) would break the confounding, and give a falsifiable within-person intervention or held-out-task evaluation. Prefer public Grand Challenge data if obtainable, but never fill a missing calibration with invented values. Explicitly account for person clustering, activity imbalance, early stance and half-cycle placebo. Quantify the minimum effect size/sample size under stated assumptions and make the assumptions visible.

Falsifier: if the proposed observable does not change the conditional distribution of *calibrated* compression given N1g inputs, or a matched placebo yields the same gain, the new method fails. A protocol without a defensible calibrated force endpoint must end UNKNOWN for empirical gain. Report exact source URLs and what they establish.
