# Public segmented-cell source note (locally checked before launch)

CellMap public bucket conventions: https://janelia-cellmap.github.io/cellmap-schemas/cellmap-conventions/s3-overview/ . The public `s3://janelia-cosem-datasets/jrc_hela-2` prefix contains the dataset. Primary publications: Heinrich et al., *Whole-cell organelle segmentation in volume electron microscopy*, Nature 599:141–146 (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41586-021-03977-3, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03977-3 ; Xu et al., *An open-access volume electron microscopy atlas of whole cells and tissues*, Nature 599:147–151 (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41586-021-03992-4, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03992-4 . Wave-2 exploration reported that ground-truth crop8 had no mito chunks; choose a label-positive crop by listing actual public objects before freezing a primary crop. Verify voxel scale and label semantics from dataset metadata; do not infer them from paper prose.
