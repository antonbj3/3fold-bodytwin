# Public-source context and open blocker

- A364 (BodyTwin register, 24/9) reported that Fmax/Lopt/tendon slack fitted to isometric and 60°/s knee strength had effective rank only 2–3/3 in extension and 2–3/6 in flexion; held-out 90°/s median curve errors were JW 33.8%, DM 94%, SC 136%. Those fits used a simplified straight muscle path and separate local model assumptions. This is a high-level outcome summary, **not** a private matrix, trajectory, force solution or parameter table. The old method failed; new EMG-informed method must test its own held-out gain against a strong simple baseline.
- Previous CLOUD-W6-STRENGTH-ID used SC Biodex angle/torque only, with a parameterized strength curve. It failed ≥10% held-out gain and found muscle-parameter sensitivity rank 3/8 (one speed), 4/8 (two speeds). Its own report mistakenly called EMG absent despite a prefiltered vaslat column; this bundle supplies separate raw multichannel EMG.
- `prior/DM_RESULTS.md`, `JW_RESULTS.md`, `SC_RESULTS.md` are observation packages and help interpret segment masks. Their implant-load channels were not carried over: their force axes/calibration are uncertain. 90°/s torque is a *real measured holdout*; 90°/s EMG is an observed covariate and may be used without its torque.
- Grand Challenge overview: DOI 10.1002/jor.22023, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4067494/ . That paper describes public raw and synchronized data, but its strength-data table refers to JW and is not a direct inventory of these DM/SC extracts. The copied CSVs themselves were rechecked by each local package's `load_test.py` before launch and listed in MANIFEST.sha256; source archive hashes are in those packages' `REQUIREMENT.json`. This is three individuals, not a population sample. Raw EMG has no calibrated activation or MVC scale, so explicit multiplicative gauges must be assessed.
- CSV parsing caveat: Biodex data rows have one trailing empty field beyond the header. With pandas, use `read_csv(..., index_col=False)` or an explicit schema; otherwise the time column silently becomes the index and values shift under the wrong labels. A pre-launch eligibility check looked only at finite values, angle span and velocity (not torque magnitudes): at 80% nominal-speed threshold, both directions in all 3 persons have 9–11 distinct 10° angle bins and roughly 78–99° spans at both speeds. Thus the frozen 5-bin/50° minimum appears feasible, subject to detailed cycle and EMG overlap checks.
