## 2. Chains that already run end-to-end (executed, with evidence)

1. **Drill sequence → thermal dose → bone damage → canal risk** (K3). The chain S0 → pilot 2,0 → 2,8 → 3,5 mm runs with SDF, T field and CEM43 on 246 sites in 40 real TF2 cases. The damage zone (CEM43 ≥ 16 min) is 0 mm with irrigation and 0,15 mm without. The canal wall is heated by at most +0,3 °C (p95). Canal risk is calculated on 2581 sites. *Caveat:* heat magnitude is REFUTED (×3,5 too low). The conclusion "canal risk is geometric, not thermal" stands even after the correction. (K3 REPORT §1–4)
2. **Drilled hole → insertion → primary stability → micromotion → design choice.** K3's final drill (Df, L) goes into PROC_insert and gives IT and p0. p0 calibrates the preload in MICRO_motion. The linkage to DESIGN was run on 370 sites in 53 cases (SOL6-recovery, identical to DESIGN final.jsonl). The necrosis condition binds: 99,7 % of the best anchorage choices violate it. *Caveat:* IT is PASS, but micromotion is wrong by ×5,9 and cortex and cadaver fail. (PROC_insert REPORT, MICRO_motion REPORT, SOL6_DENTAL_RECOVERY REPORT)
3. **Muscle geometry → load direction → TMJ reaction → FE → crest stress.** The W3 lever arms give the condylar reaction (843 N → 650 N) in FE_REFERENCE. LOAD_mastication gives the θ distribution, which was swept on P17 in FE. BONE_HU repeats the sweep on voxel-FE with the patient's own HU field (2 TF2 patients; direction explains 72 % and 82 %). (FE_REFERENCE, LOAD_mastication, BONE_HU REPORT)
4. **PDL law → FE → orthodontic speed** (F1 → A227 → ORTHO_rate). The chain was run on 8 teeth. The result is REFUTED because PDL pressure saturates at clinical forces.
5. **Crestal cortex (BONE_PV) + load spectrum + tooth reference (F1) → harmonic FE → mechanostat → MBL after 5 years** (REMODEL). The chain was run in 37 scenarios. The biological part (L2) passes 2/5 contrasts, and the mechanics term is REFUTED.
6. **Load spectrum → surface process → SCG → crown's 5-years risk** (LOAD_spectrum → SURF → SCG_crown). PASS against the clinical bands, but rests on 2 assumed θ.
7. **FatigueData → Murakami → Kt at thread root → ISO 14801 assembly → F_lim** (MAT_PSP → K1 → K1b). The chain was run against 18 anchors. The prediction becomes ≈ 0,61× the measured value and the miss lies on the strength side.
8. **Preparation → RDT → pulp temperature** (PROC_prep). Run on a real STS molar in three steps. Sign and order match (T2 PASS), magnitude does not (T1 FAIL).

No chain runs all the way patient → lifetime. The longest connected one is no. 2 (geometry → drill → insertion → micromotion → design choice). It ends before biology and lifetime.
