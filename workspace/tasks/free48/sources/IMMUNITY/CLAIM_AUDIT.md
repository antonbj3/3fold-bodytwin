# Claim audit — supplied mitochondria/stress post

The seed is useful; its strongest assertions are research questions.

- “More energy than running a mile”: not established by the identified review. Require matched duration, mass, activity and net whole-body calorimetry. A cellular energy-cost result cannot be scaled directly to a person.
- Cortisol production spans mitochondria and endoplasmic reticulum in steroidogenic cells. Specify StAR transport, mitochondrial and ER enzymes and substrate/redox supply.
- The review discusses outer-membrane monoamine oxidases in catecholamine degradation. It does not establish a general mitochondrial adrenaline-synthesis pathway.
- Acute and chronic effects vary by tissue, exposure, dose and measurement. Capacity, demand and actual flux are different quantities.
- Glycolysis contributes ATP; cell types differ. Mitochondrial signaling requires an explicit source, transport/target and clearance mechanism.
- Depression/metabolic associations do not alone identify causation. Sex and age effects need cell/tissue-specific evidence and composition control.
