# Primary sources and evidence boundaries

## M01 — Picard et al. 2018, An energetic view of stress

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5964020/

Review. Mitochondrial energetics, stress signaling and organelle plasticity; no universal mile-running energy comparison established.

## M02 — Picard et al. 2015, mitochondrial perturbation and stress

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4672794/

Mouse genetic perturbations; retain species and intervention scope.

## M03 — Basarrate et al. 2024, stress receptor atlas

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10922488/

Tissue receptor heterogeneity; transcript/protein abundance does not determine functional flux.

## M04 — Picard and McEwen 2018, systematic review

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29389736/

Historical animal and human evidence; acute/chronic effects require tissue, dose and time context.

## I01 — Norelli et al. 2018, CAR-T cytokines and neurotoxicity

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29808007/

Humanized mice: monocyte IL-1/IL-6 mechanisms differ; blocking IL-6R prevented CRS in the experiment but did not prevent delayed neurotoxicity.

## I02 — Giavridis et al. 2018, macrophage-mediated CAR-T CRS

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-018-0041-7

Experimental macrophage IL-1/IL-6/NO contribution. Do not represent every cytokine as CAR-T-derived.

## I03 — Axelrod et al. 2022, myosin-specific T cells

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36385524/

Antigen-specific myocarditis mechanism in the studied mouse setting with selected human observations.

## I04 — Blum et al. 2024, heart/blood/tumour immunity

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39506125/

Human tissue study: 52 heart-expanded clones from eight patients did not recognize tested myosin/troponin antigens. Preserve this boundary against universal myosin mechanisms.

## I05 — Sasson et al. 2021, tissue-resident cells in ICI colitis

https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8527886/

Human tissue/cell evidence for activated CD8 resident cells and IFN-gamma; compartment and sampling matter.

## I06 — Zou et al. 2021, vedolizumab/infliximab observations

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34789551/

Two-centre observational comparison; treatment selection and other confounding require explicit handling.

## I07 — Salem et al. 2023, abatacept/ruxolitinib strategy

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36815259/

Combined immunomodulation and respiratory management strategy; component effects are not randomized isolated causal estimates.

## I08 — Sivan et al. 2015, commensals and checkpoint response

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26541606/

Mouse commensal perturbation; no generic human probiotic efficacy parameter follows.

## I09 — Popel laboratory QSP implementation

https://github.com/popellab/QSPIO-TNBC

Existing QSP platform to inspect for equations, compartments, code license and applicable baselines; not yet installed or reproduced here.

## I10 — QSP virtual trial, hepatocellular carcinoma 2022

https://jitc.bmj.com/content/10/11/e005414

Published mechanistic model, equations and code reference; virtual-cohort agreement does not validate unseen organ toxicity.

## I11 — NCI cancer immunotherapy

https://www.cancer.gov/about-cancer/treatment/types/immunotherapy

Official context on modalities and effects on healthy tissue; not a quantitative mechanistic calibration source.
