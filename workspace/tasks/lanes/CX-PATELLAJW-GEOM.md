# CX-PATELLAJW-GEOM — why does JW's patellar tendon have no attainable L1 length root at 40–60°?

Source: results/CX-PATELLAJW/RESULTS.md. In a source-exact JW1 gait reconstruction, all six 40–60° implant-mask frames lack a patella-hinge angle satisfying the neutral tendon length within 3 mm; the best possible residual is 4.6–8.4 mm, while the tendon moment arm is 4–5 mm. A frozen published-point force-ratio replacement changes A[3], cj and Mkx consistently but leaves 48.64% of JW gait below LP lo; all three criteria fail. The branch does not jump in the sampled trial. This is an unresolved geometric incompatibility, not proof of a specific wrong attachment.

Before any implant comparison, preregister and hash an independent geometry check.
