## Method and default selection

AGENTS.md, START.md, FIRST_PRINCIPLES.md, the excavation workflow and all five source batches were read. All 500 Markdown lines matched the canonical JSON program without deviation. INITIAL_JOBS, STATE and CATALOG were read as execution context; the job directories were checked separately. `./graph working rank` and seven `working packet` founded the definitions. Exact packets/hashes is in `results/LANE_RUNNER_IMPROVE_500/graph_packets/`; they are a curated working view, not complete native dependencies.

For each mutation, a new operation, observable measure, adverse construction, strong control pool, reject rule and consumer were written individually. We test decision adequacy rather than assuming that proxies provide a full state/causal/physics certificate. T41/T43/T44/T45/T48 are coupled via specified operator/observation/composition/fusion steps and ablation requirements, only as suggested method couplings.

First principles contain constituents/state, interaction, conditionally derived relation, four scoped leaves with provenance and stop arguments, reconstruction/feedback and error budget. **2 000 leaves**: 500 `DERIVED_UNDER_ASSUMPTIONS`, 500 `CONSTITUTIVE_CLOSURE`, 1 000 `UNKNOWN`. No `EXTERNALLY_MEASURED` leaves were created without actual measurements. Separate `results/LANE_RUNNER_IMPROVE_500/decompositions/M*/DECOMPOSITION.json` reflect these definitions; targets/stress chains are in `results/LANE_RUNNER_IMPROVE_500/GOAL_TO_STRESS_POINT.md`. Five 100 records intermediate results, theme/domain/baseline/test map, authored individual lines and reproducible builder were also saved.

Accuracy and complete setup+tuning+discovery+validation+update are matched; memory, CPU/GPU time, assays and communication are reported separately. Strongest eligible supplied implementation is chosen before holdout; v6 specifies relevant strong candidate methods but **does not** determine globally strongest current method through this local review. Numeric standards are design choices: reuse N=1,10,100; synthetic window t/τ∈[0,10]. Physical τ, actual sampling horizon, assaynoise, ε_y and practical decision tolerance must be bound before empirical test. Ambition ≥2× lower completecost or new query capability is a search bar, never measurement data or positive report requirement. If calibration is missing, a precise prerequisite is left.
